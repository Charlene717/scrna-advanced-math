計數分布：從 Poisson 到 ZINB

Count Distributions: from Poisson to ZINB

打開一個 10x 矩陣，看 CD3D 這個基因在 1000 個細胞中的 count：大多是 0、有些是 1-3、少數高達 50。 這種「多 0、長尾」的分布形狀，背後的機率模型是什麼？

這節從最簡單的 Poisson 出發，互動比較 Poisson vs 實測， 發現「Poisson 不夠」（overdispersion），然後推導 Negative Binomial（NB）為 Poisson-Gamma mixture， 最後談 Zero-Inflated NB（ZINB）是不是必要——以及為什麼現代共識是「NB 已經夠了」。
Open a 10x matrix, look at CD3D's counts across 1000 cells: mostly 0, some 1–3, occasional 50. What probabilistic model generates this "many zeros, long tail" shape?

We start from Poisson, compare it interactively to empirical data, discover Poisson isn't enough (overdispersion), derive Negative Binomial as Poisson-Gamma mixture, then discuss Zero-Inflated NB (ZINB) — and why the modern consensus is "NB suffices".

§1 Poisson：第一個合理模型Poisson: first reasonable model

Poisson 是最古典的「計數分布」。想像一個 mRNA 池子，每個 mRNA 分子被捕捉的機率很小， 但分子數很多——這正是 Poisson 適用的場景（「rare events」極限）。

Poisson is the classical "count distribution". Imagine an mRNA pool: each molecule has tiny capture probability, but there are many molecules — exactly Poisson's regime (rare-events limit).

1.1 PMF

1.1 PMF

$$P(X = k) = \frac{\lambda^k e^{-\lambda}}{k!}, \quad k = 0, 1, 2, \dots$$
$\lambda$ 是平均（也是變異）
$\lambda$ is the mean (and also the variance)

1.2 性質

1.2 Properties

  • $E[X] = \lambda$
  • $\text{Var}(X) = \lambda$
  • 兩 Poisson 相加：Sum of Poissons: $X_1 + X_2 \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda_1 + \lambda_2)$

§2 為什麼 Poisson 不夠：overdispersionWhy Poisson fails: overdispersion

Poisson 預測 variance = mean。但實測 scRNA-seq 永遠 variance > mean，差距常常 5-50 倍。這就是 overdispersion。

Poisson predicts variance = mean. Empirically, scRNA-seq always shows variance > mean, often 5–50× off. This is overdispersion.

2.1 為什麼會有 overdispersion?

2.1 Why does overdispersion happen?

原因Cause 說明Explanation
細胞型態混合Cell-type mixture CD3D 在 T 細胞高、其他細胞低 → 混在一起看就 overdispersed CD3D high in T cells, low elsewhere → pooled distribution is overdispersed
細胞間生物變異Cell-to-cell biological variation 即使同型細胞，每個細胞「實際」基因表現也不同 Even same type, cells truly vary in expression
技術變異（capture）Technical (capture) variation 每細胞 capture efficiency 不同 Capture efficiency varies cell-to-cell

這三件事都可以歸納成：「每個細胞的真實 $\lambda$ 不一樣」。Poisson 假設所有細胞共用同一個 $\lambda$，所以低估變異。

All three reduce to: "each cell has a different true $\lambda$". Poisson assumes one shared $\lambda$ — underestimating variance.

§3 NB 的推導：Poisson-Gamma mixtureNB derivation: Poisson-Gamma mixture

如果每個細胞的 $\lambda_i$ 來自一個分布而不是固定常數，就會自然產生 overdispersion。 數學家發現：如果 $\lambda$ 來自 Gamma 分布，整個合成分布剛好就是 Negative Binomial。

If each cell's $\lambda_i$ comes from a distribution rather than a fixed constant, overdispersion emerges naturally. Mathematically: when $\lambda$ has a Gamma distribution, the marginal becomes exactly Negative Binomial.

$$\lambda \sim \text{Gamma}(\theta, \theta/\mu), \quad X | \lambda \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X \sim \text{NB}(\mu, \theta)$$
Poisson-Gamma mixture 就是 NB（也叫 Gamma-Poisson）
Poisson-Gamma mixture = NB (a.k.a. Gamma-Poisson)

3.1 NB 的 PMF

3.1 NB PMF

$$P(X=k) = \frac{\Gamma(k+\theta)}{k! \, \Gamma(\theta)} \left(\frac{\theta}{\theta+\mu}\right)^\theta \left(\frac{\mu}{\theta+\mu}\right)^k$$

3.2 性質

3.2 Properties

  • $E[X] = \mu$
  • $\text{Var}(X) = \mu + \mu^2 / \theta$ → 第二項就是 overdispersion 的來源→ second term is the overdispersion
  • $\theta \to \infty$ 時退回 Poisson$\theta \to \infty$ recovers Poisson
  • $\theta$ 越小 → overdispersion 越大Smaller $\theta$ → more overdispersion
▸ 一個簡化推導（積分掉 λ） ▸ Sketch derivation (integrate out λ)

$P(X=k) = \int_0^\infty P(X=k|\lambda) \, p(\lambda) \, d\lambda$

= $\int_0^\infty \frac{\lambda^k e^{-\lambda}}{k!} \cdot \frac{(\theta/\mu)^\theta}{\Gamma(\theta)} \lambda^{\theta-1} e^{-\theta \lambda/\mu} \, d\lambda$

= $\frac{(\theta/\mu)^\theta}{k! \, \Gamma(\theta)} \int_0^\infty \lambda^{k+\theta-1} e^{-\lambda(1 + \theta/\mu)} \, d\lambda$

= $\frac{(\theta/\mu)^\theta}{k! \, \Gamma(\theta)} \cdot \frac{\Gamma(k+\theta)}{(1 + \theta/\mu)^{k+\theta}}$

整理 → 上面那個 NB PMF。✓

$P(X=k) = \int_0^\infty P(X=k|\lambda) \, p(\lambda) \, d\lambda$

= $\frac{(\theta/\mu)^\theta}{k! \, \Gamma(\theta)} \int_0^\infty \lambda^{k+\theta-1} e^{-\lambda(1 + \theta/\mu)} \, d\lambda$

= $\frac{(\theta/\mu)^\theta}{k! \, \Gamma(\theta)} \cdot \frac{\Gamma(k+\theta)}{(1+\theta/\mu)^{k+\theta}}$

Rearrange → NB PMF above. ✓

§5 Dropout：為什麼 0 看起來「太多」？Dropout: why "too many" zeros?

早期 scRNA-seq 文獻常說「scRNA 有 dropout」——意指「有些基因明明該有表現，但因為技術問題（mRNA 降解、reverse transcription 失敗）count 變成 0」。 這多出來的 0 不是「真正沒表現」，是「技術上漏掉」。

Early scRNA literature talks about "dropout" — genes that should be expressed but show 0 counts due to technical issues (mRNA degradation, failed reverse transcription). These extra zeros aren't "truly unexpressed", they're "technically missed".

5.1 數學模型：Zero-inflation

5.1 Math: zero-inflation

$$X = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{with probability } \pi \quad (\text{dropout}) \\ Y & \text{with probability } 1 - \pi, \quad Y \sim \text{NB}(\mu, \theta) \end{cases}$$
$\pi$ = dropout 機率，多出來的「技術 0」
$\pi$ = dropout probability, extra "technical zeros"

5.2 PMF

5.2 PMF

$$P(X=0) = \pi + (1-\pi) \cdot P_{\text{NB}}(0), \quad P(X=k>0) = (1-\pi) \cdot P_{\text{NB}}(k)$$

§7 現代共識：UMI 資料用 NB 就好Modern consensus: NB suffices for UMI

ZINB 在 2014-2017 年的早期 scRNA 文獻中很流行（Pierson & Yau ZIFA, ZinbWave 等）。 但 2019 之後 Hafemeister & Satija (scTransform)、Townes (GLM-PCA) 等多篇論文發現： 對 UMI-based 資料（10x、Drop-seq），NB 已經夠了。

ZINB was popular in early scRNA literature (2014–2017): Pierson & Yau ZIFA, ZinbWave, etc. Post-2019, Hafemeister & Satija (scTransform), Townes (GLM-PCA), and others showed: for UMI-based data (10x, Drop-seq), NB suffices.

7.1 為什麼 UMI 不需要 ZINB?

7.1 Why doesn't UMI need ZINB?

關鍵理解Key insight: 所謂的「dropout」在非 UMI 平台（Smart-seq2 等）是因為 PCR amplification 的隨機性造成的——某些分子沒被 amplify、其他大量 amplify。 UMI 平台（10x 等）每個分子帶獨一無二的 barcode，PCR 後去重，所以「PCR dropout」這個來源根本不存在。剩下的「0」就是真正的「沒表現 + Poisson noise」——NB 完美擬合。 "Dropout" in non-UMI platforms (Smart-seq2) comes from PCR amplification randomness — some molecules don't amplify, others over-amplify. In UMI platforms (10x), each molecule has a unique barcode; PCR is deduplicated post-hoc — PCR dropout simply doesn't exist. Remaining zeros are genuinely "unexpressed + Poisson noise" — NB fits perfectly.

7.2 實證：NB vs ZINB 擬合 10x 資料

7.2 Evidence: NB vs ZINB fit to 10x data

在 Hafemeister & Satija 2019 的實驗：對 33000 個 10x cells × 22000 genes 同時 fit NB 跟 ZINB。 結果：99.5% 的 gene，NB 跟 ZINB 的 likelihood 幾乎一樣（差 < 0.5%）。多出 ZINB 的 π 參數沒幫助。

In Hafemeister & Satija 2019: fit NB and ZINB to 33k 10x cells × 22k genes. Result: for 99.5% of genes, NB and ZINB likelihoods differ < 0.5%. The extra π parameter doesn't help.

§10 完整手算：對單一基因 fit NB GLMWorked example: fit NB GLM to a single gene

Pearson residuals 跟 scTransform 的核心是「對每個 gene 跑一個 NB GLM」。 本節對 CD3D 用 8 個合成細胞做完整手算，讓你看到 GLM fit 的每一步。

Pearson residuals and scTransform's core is "fit an NB GLM per gene". We walk a full hand-calculation for CD3D on 8 synthetic cells.

10.1 模型設定

10.1 Model setup

$$X_{ij} \sim \text{NB}(\mu_{ij}, \theta_j), \quad \log \mu_{ij} = \beta_{0,j} + \beta_{1,j} \log s_i$$
$s_i$ = cell $i$ 的 total counts (size factor)
$s_i$ = cell $i$'s total counts (size factor)

直覺：CD3D 的表現量 $\mu$ 應該跟細胞 depth $s_i$ 成比例（depth 高 → 多偵測幾個分子）。 GLM 把這個關係用對數線性建模——這正是 size factor normalization 的「機率版」。

Intuition: CD3D's expected count $\mu$ should be proportional to cell depth $s_i$ (deeper → more molecules detected). GLM models this log-linearly — a "probabilistic" version of size factor normalization.

10.3 求解 $\beta_0, \beta_1$（IRLS 迭代）

10.3 Solve $\beta_0, \beta_1$ (IRLS iteration)

NB GLM 用 iteratively reweighted least squares (IRLS) 求解。每步迭代用 working response + working weights 做加權 OLS：

NB GLM uses iteratively reweighted least squares (IRLS). Each iteration does weighted OLS with working response + weights:

  1. 初始：Init: $\beta_0^{(0)} = \log \bar{x} - \beta_1^{(0)} \overline{\log s} = -5.55$, $\beta_1^{(0)} = 1.0$
  2. 計算 fitted：Compute fitted: $\hat\mu_i = \exp(\beta_0 + \beta_1 \log s_i)$
  3. working response：Working response: $z_i = \log \hat\mu_i + (x_i - \hat\mu_i)/\hat\mu_i$
  4. working weights：Working weights: $w_i = \hat\mu_i^2 / (\hat\mu_i + \hat\mu_i^2/\theta)$（NB variance 的倒數）
  5. 加權 OLS：Weighted OLS: 對 $(z_i, \log s_i, w_i)$ 做迴歸，更新 $\beta$
  6. 迭代 5-10 次至收斂Iterate 5–10× until convergence
$$\hat{\beta}_0 = -8.93, \quad \hat{\beta}_1 = 1.65, \quad \hat{\theta} = 12.4$$

§11 深入：Pearson residual 為何「自動歸一化」變異Deep dive: why Pearson residuals "auto-normalize" variance

你已經看到 Pearson residual 公式 $z = (x - \mu)/\sigma$。但「為什麼 PCA 餵這個會比餵 log 更好」這個直覺， 需要 GLM 的方差函數 (variance function) 跟「variance-stabilizing transform」的概念才完整。本節打開這個黑盒。

You've seen $z = (x - \mu)/\sigma$. But the intuition "why feeding this to PCA beats feeding log(x+1)" requires GLM's variance function and "variance-stabilizing transform" concepts. We open this box.

11.1 Variance function 跟「全部 gene σ=1」的關係

11.1 Variance function and "all genes σ=1"

在 GLM 中，每個分布家族有自己的 variance function $V(\mu)$，定義為 $\text{Var}(X) = V(\mu) \cdot \phi$，$\phi$ 是 dispersion。 Poisson: $V(\mu) = \mu$；NB: $V(\mu) = \mu + \mu^2/\theta$；Normal: $V(\mu) = 1$。

In GLM, each distribution family has a variance function $V(\mu)$ where $\text{Var}(X) = V(\mu) \cdot \phi$, $\phi$ = dispersion. Poisson: $V(\mu) = \mu$; NB: $V(\mu) = \mu + \mu^2/\theta$; Normal: $V(\mu) = 1$.

$$\text{Var}(z_i) = \text{Var}\!\left(\frac{X_i - \mu_i}{\sqrt{V(\mu_i)}}\right) = \frac{\text{Var}(X_i)}{V(\mu_i)} = \frac{V(\mu_i)}{V(\mu_i)} = 1$$
標準化操作把每個 gene 的變異「拉到 1」
Standardization pulls every gene's variance to 1

這就是「Pearson residual 把所有 gene σ 拉到 1」的數學原因——不是經驗法則，是GLM 變異函數的數學必然。

This is the math reason "Pearson residuals pull all genes' σ to 1" — not an empirical rule, but a mathematical necessity from GLM's variance function.

11.2 Variance-stabilizing transform 的角度

11.2 Variance-stabilizing transform perspective

另一個觀點：對 NB 變數，存在一個函數 $g$ 使得 $g(X)$ 變異不再依賴於 $\mu$。 Anscombe (1948) 推導出 NB 的 VST：

Alternative view: for NB, there exists a function $g$ such that $g(X)$'s variance is independent of $\mu$. Anscombe (1948) derived NB's VST:

$$g(x) = \sinh^{-1}\!\sqrt{x \cdot \theta / (\theta + 1)} \cdot \sqrt{4 + 2/\theta}$$
Anscombe (1948)。$\theta \to \infty$ 退回 Poisson VST：$g(x) = 2\sqrt{x}$
Anscombe (1948). $\theta \to \infty$ recovers Poisson VST: $g(x) = 2\sqrt{x}$

11.3 為什麼 Pearson residual 比 VST 流行

11.3 Why Pearson beats VST in practice

VST 只 stabilize 變異、不中心化；Pearson 同時 centring + standardize。直接餵 PCA 不需另做 mean-subtract。實作上一行解決，這是 scTransform 廣為採用的關鍵。 VST only stabilizes variance, doesn't centre; Pearson does both. Feeds PCA without extra mean-subtraction. One-line implementation — key reason scTransform took off.

§12 推廣：CITE-seq protein 跟 ATAC peaks 的分布Extension: distributions for CITE-seq protein and ATAC peaks

scRNA-seq 只是「單細胞多體學 (multiomics)」的開始。本節快速看 CITE-seq（同時測 RNA + 表面 protein）跟 scATAC-seq（測染色質可及性）的分布特性，知道每個 modality 該用什麼模型。

scRNA-seq is just the start of single-cell multiomics. We quickly cover CITE-seq (RNA + surface protein) and scATAC-seq (chromatin accessibility) distributions, so you know which model fits which modality.

12.1 CITE-seq：ADT (antibody-derived tags)

12.1 CITE-seq: ADT (antibody-derived tags)

CITE-seq（Stoeckius et al. 2017）在 scRNA 同時測 ~100 個表面 protein 的相對量。 protein count 的分布有兩個有趣特性：

CITE-seq (Stoeckius et al. 2017) measures ~100 surface proteins alongside scRNA. Protein count distributions have two interesting properties:

  • 沒有 dropout：protein 是用 antibody capture（不需 PCR amplification deep noise）→ 0 比 RNA 少很多
  • No dropout: proteins captured by antibody (no PCR deep amp noise) → far fewer zeros than RNA
  • 背景訊號高：每個細胞都有一些非特異 antibody binding 造成 baseline
  • High background: every cell has some non-specific antibody binding baseline
$$X_{\text{ADT}, ij} \sim \text{NB}(\mu_{ij}^{\text{specific}} + \mu_{ij}^{\text{background}}, \theta)$$
兩個 NB 加總：真實訊號 + 背景
Two NBs summed: true signal + background

推薦工具：DSB normalization (Mulè et al. 2022) — 用 empty droplets 估計 background，扣掉後再做下游。

Recommended: DSB normalization (Mulè et al. 2022) — estimate background from empty droplets, subtract before downstream.

12.2 scATAC-seq：peak count 跟 binary 化

12.2 scATAC-seq: peak counts and binarization

scATAC-seq 測「染色質開放」的位置（peaks）。每個 cell × peak 的計數有非常極端的特性：

scATAC-seq measures chromatin "open" sites (peaks). Each cell × peak count is extreme:

  • 大部分是 0 或 1：peak 通常只有 0、1、2 個 reads，分布很「壓平」
  • Mostly 0 or 1: peaks usually have 0, 1, or 2 reads — heavily flat
  • 實務上 binary 化：count > 0 設 1、否則 0。等於用 Bernoulli 分布
  • Often binarized: count > 0 → 1, else 0 — equivalent to Bernoulli distribution
