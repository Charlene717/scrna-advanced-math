Diffusion Maps：流形上的隨機漫步距離

Diffusion Maps: random-walk distance on a manifold

PCA、t-SNE、UMAP 都用「歐式距離」當高維距離。 但細胞 A 跟細胞 B 的歐式直線可能穿過資料根本不存在的區域——這個距離反映不了真實流形上的距離。

Diffusion Maps（Coifman & Lafon 2006）改用「隨機漫步」當距離： 從 A 走 t 步隨機漫步，到 B 的機率有多大？走得越通暢，距離越近。 這特別適合連續分化軌跡——本節是 DPT pseudotime 的數學基礎。
PCA, t-SNE, UMAP all use Euclidean distance in high-D. But the straight line from cell A to B may pass through empty regions — this distance doesn't reflect the true manifold distance.

Diffusion Maps (Coifman & Lafon 2006) use a "random walk" distance: starting random walk from A for $t$ steps, how likely to reach B? Easier paths → closer. Especially suited for continuous differentiation trajectories — this section is the math foundation of DPT pseudotime.

🧬 scRNA-seq 視角：細胞分化像爬山，不能走直線 🧬 scRNA-seq perspective: cell differentiation is mountaineering — you can't take a straight line

想像你家跟對街朋友家直線距離只有 20 公尺，但中間隔著一棟大樓——你不能穿牆、不能飛簷走壁，得繞過大樓走 200 公尺才到。「直線距離」騙你、「實際走路距離」才真實——這就是 scRNA 細胞分化的場景：HSC（造血幹細胞）跟成熟紅血球在 PCA 空間「直線距離」看起來很近，但生物上你必須經過 MEP → proerythroblast 這條沿著資料密集的「路」，不能瞬移過空蕩蕩的「資料空洞」。 Imagine your house and a friend's house are only 20 m apart straight-line, but a building stands between them — you can't pass through walls or fly, so you walk 200 m around the building. "Straight-line distance" lies, "actual walking distance" tells the truth — exactly the scRNA differentiation scenario: HSC (stem cell) and mature erythrocyte look "straight-line close" in PCA space, but biologically you must pass through MEP → proerythroblast along the data-dense "road" — no teleporting across empty "data voids".

Diffusion Maps 就是「沿著資料密集的路」算距離。它派出一個隨機漫步者，每步只能跳到鄰居（有東西的地方），算「從 A 到 B 平均要走幾步」——這就是 diffusion distance。Coifman & Lafon 2006 提出，Haghverdi 2016 把它帶進 scRNA。它對 dropout 和 noise 特別魯棒，因為「多步漫步」是機率平均，單一 outlier cell 影響不了大局。所有你聽過的 trajectory 工具——DPT、Palantir、PAGA、Monocle 3、CellRank——底盤全是 diffusion。沒它，scRNA 永遠只能說「有什麼細胞」，說不出「細胞怎麼變」。 Diffusion Maps measures distance "along data-dense paths". It sends out a random walker who can only hop between neighbors (filled spaces), then asks "on average, how many steps to walk from A to B" — that's diffusion distance. Coifman & Lafon 2006 proposed it; Haghverdi 2016 brought it to scRNA. It's extra robust to dropout and noise because multi-step walks are probabilistic averages — one outlier cell doesn't derail the whole journey. Every trajectory tool you've heard of — DPT, Palantir, PAGA, Monocle 3, CellRank — runs on diffusion underneath. Without it, scRNA can only say "what cells exist", never "how cells transform".

Diffusion Maps is the mathematical key that upgrades scRNA-seq from "static snapshot" to "dynamic movie".

§0 故事：歐式距離 vs 流形距離Story: Euclidean vs manifold distance

想像細胞分布在 2D 平面上一條「S 型曲線」上。細胞 A 在 S 上方、細胞 B 在 S 下方。 歐式距離 |A−B| 看起來很近（直線距離小）——但沿著 S 曲線從 A 走到 B 要繞很遠。 分化軌跡上 A 跟 B 之間有大量中間細胞連通；如果你直接用歐式距離，會錯誤地把 A、B 當成「鄰居」。

Imagine cells on a 2D "S-shaped curve". Cell A on top of S, B on bottom. Euclidean |A−B| seems close (small straight-line distance) — but along the S curve, A to B is a long path. Differentiation trajectories have many intermediate cells linking A to B; Euclidean wrongly calls A and B "neighbors".

§0-1 Visual contrast

關鍵直覺Key intuition: 流形距離 = 「沿著實際資料分布走需要多遠」。Diffusion Maps 用隨機漫步機率定義這個距離，自動跟著「資料密集的路徑」走。 Manifold distance = "distance along the actual data distribution". Diffusion Maps uses random-walk probabilities to define this, automatically following "data-dense paths".

§1 隨機漫步的直覺Random walk intuition

把每個細胞當圖上的節點，鄰近細胞之間有邊（權重 = 相似度）。 從細胞 $i$ 出發隨機漫步：每步「向最相似的鄰居」跳轉，機率正比於邊權重。 問：走 t 步後，落在細胞 $j$ 的機率是多少? 這個機率就描述了 $i$ 跟 $j$ 在流形上的「連通性」。

Treat each cell as a graph node, nearby cells linked by edges (weights = similarities). Random walk from cell $i$: each step jumps to a neighbor with probability proportional to edge weight. Question: what's the probability of landing at cell $j$ after $t$ steps? This probability captures their "connectivity" on the manifold.

為何隨機漫步Why random walk: 如果 $i$ 到 $j$ 有多條短路徑連通 → t 步後到 $j$ 的機率高；若只有一條長路徑 → 機率低。這比歐式距離更能反映流形的「實際連通性」。 If $i$ to $j$ has many short paths → high probability after $t$ steps; single long path → low. Better captures manifold "actual connectivity" than Euclidean.

§2 轉移矩陣 $T$ 的構造Transition matrix $T$

2.1 三步驟

2.1 Three steps

  1. 相似度矩陣：Similarity matrix: $K_{ij} = \exp(-\|x_i - x_j\|^2 / \sigma^2)$（Gaussian kernel）
  2. (可選) 密度校正：$\tilde{K}_{ij} = K_{ij} / (q_i q_j)^\alpha$，其中 $q_i = \sum_j K_{ij}$(Optional) density correction: $\tilde{K}_{ij} = K_{ij} / (q_i q_j)^\alpha$, where $q_i = \sum_j K_{ij}$
  3. 列正規化：Row-normalize: $T_{ij} = \tilde{K}_{ij} / d_i$，$d_i = \sum_j \tilde{K}_{ij}$
$$T_{ij} = \frac{K_{ij}}{\sum_k K_{ik}}, \quad \sum_j T_{ij} = 1$$
$T$ 是「從 $i$ 隨機跳到 $j$ 的機率」
$T$ = "probability of jumping from $i$ to $j$"

2.2 密度校正的作用

2.2 Role of density correction

$\alpha = 1$（CMM = Coifman & Maggioni）：完全消除密度影響，讓 diffusion 只反映「流形幾何」不反映「細胞密度」。 $\alpha = 0$：直接用 random walk，密集區會「主導」整個 diffusion。 scanpy / DPT 預設 $\alpha = 1$。

$\alpha = 1$ (Coifman & Maggioni): fully removes density effect — diffusion reflects only manifold geometry, not cell density. $\alpha = 0$: raw random walk, dense regions dominate. Scanpy / DPT default $\alpha = 1$.

2.3 t 步轉移

2.3 t-step transition

$$(T^t)_{ij} = P(\text{在 } t \text{ 步內從 } i \text{ 到 } j)$$
$T$ 的 t 次冪等於「t 步轉移機率」
$T^t$ = "$t$-step transition probability"

隨著 t 增大：

As $t$ grows:

  • $t = 1$：只反映「直接鄰居」
  • $t = 1$: direct neighbors only
  • $t = 10$：「沿流形 10 步」都被當「近」
  • $t = 10$: "10 steps along manifold" all count as "close"
  • $t \to \infty$：每點機率分布收斂到 stationary distribution（與起點無關）
  • $t \to \infty$: distribution converges to stationary (independent of start)

§4 特徵分解 + diffusion distanceEigen-decomp + diffusion distance

4.1 對 $T$ 做特徵分解

4.1 Eigen-decompose $T$

$$T = \Phi \Lambda \Phi^{-1}, \quad \Lambda = \text{diag}(\lambda_0, \lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_{n-1})$$
$\lambda_0 = 1$（trivial），$1 = \lambda_0 \ge |\lambda_1| \ge |\lambda_2| \ge \cdots$
$\lambda_0 = 1$ (trivial), $1 = \lambda_0 \ge |\lambda_1| \ge \cdots$

特徵向量 $\phi_k$（叫 diffusion components, DCs）就是「流形上的座標」。前幾個 DC 描述「流形的主要方向」。

Eigenvectors $\phi_k$ (called diffusion components, DCs) are "coordinates on the manifold". First few DCs describe "manifold's main directions".

4.2 Diffusion distance

4.2 Diffusion distance

$$D_t^2(i, j) = \sum_{k \ge 1} \lambda_k^{2t} (\phi_k(i) - \phi_k(j))^2$$
在 diffusion space 的歐式距離，但每維被 $\lambda_k^t$ 加權
Euclidean in diffusion space, each dim weighted by $\lambda_k^t$

$\lambda_k^t$ 隨 $t$ 衰減：大 $t$ → 高頻 DC 被壓掉、只剩低頻（流形大尺度）。 DPT 對應一個特殊的距離（不直接用上面公式，但同源）。

$\lambda_k^t$ decays with $t$: large $t$ → high-frequency DCs suppressed, only low-freq (manifold large-scale) remain. DPT corresponds to a special distance (not exactly the formula above, but same family).

4.3 降維：取前 k 個 DC 當座標

4.3 Reduce: top $k$ DCs as coordinates

$$y_i = (\lambda_1^t \phi_1(i), \lambda_2^t \phi_2(i), \ldots, \lambda_k^t \phi_k(i))$$

通常取 k=2 或 3 畫 plot，類似 PCA 但對非線性流形更敏感。對連續分化軌跡特別好。

Typically $k=2$ or $3$ for plotting — PCA-like but more sensitive to nonlinear manifolds. Excellent for continuous differentiation.

§6 與 DPT 的連接Connection to DPT

DPT (Diffusion Pseudotime) 直接基於 diffusion maps：給 root 細胞 $i_0$，pseudotime = $D_\infty(i_0, i)$（$t \to \infty$ 的 diffusion 距離）。

DPT (Diffusion Pseudotime) directly builds on diffusion maps: given root $i_0$, pseudotime = $D_\infty(i_0, i)$ ($t \to \infty$ diffusion distance).

$$\text{dpt}(i_0, i) = \sqrt{\sum_{k \ge 1} \left(\frac{\lambda_k}{1 - \lambda_k}\right)^2 (\phi_k(i_0) - \phi_k(i))^2}$$

$\lambda_k / (1 - \lambda_k)$ 是「accumulated diffusion」的修正項——避免 $t \to \infty$ 時所有資訊壓平到 stationary distribution。

$\lambda_k / (1 - \lambda_k)$ is the "accumulated diffusion" correction — avoids info being flattened to stationary as $t \to \infty$.

§9 PHATE 跟 SPRING：diffusion 思想的進階版PHATE and SPRING: advanced variants of diffusion

Diffusion Maps 啟發了一批「軌跡視覺化」工具。本節介紹兩個重要分支：PHATE（Moon 2019）跟 SPRING（Weinreb 2018）。

Diffusion Maps inspired a family of "trajectory visualization" tools. We cover two important branches: PHATE (Moon 2019) and SPRING (Weinreb 2018).

9.1 PHATE：Potential of Heat-diffusion for Affinity-based Trajectory Embedding

9.1 PHATE: Potential of Heat-diffusion for Affinity-based Trajectory Embedding

PHATE 的關鍵創新：用「potential distance」取代 diffusion distance。對 transition matrix $T$ 算多 t 步後對 log 取 distance：

PHATE's key innovation: replace diffusion distance with "potential distance". On transition matrix $T$, take log distance after multi-step:

$$D_{\text{potential}}(i, j) = \| \log T^t_{i,:} - \log T^t_{j,:} \|$$
$\log$ 讓低機率 entries（遠距離）也有意義，不被 $t \to \infty$ 拉平
$\log$ keeps low-probability entries (long range) meaningful, not flattened by $t \to \infty$

優勢：對連續、分支軌跡視覺效果好，是 single-cell trajectory 教學最常用的 plot。

Advantage: visually excellent for continuous, branching trajectories — common in single-cell trajectory tutorials.

關鍵關係：$L_{\text{norm}}$ 跟 $T$ 的特徵值互補：

11.1 PAGA 演算法

11.1 PAGA algorithm

  1. 用 Leiden 分群Leiden cluster
  2. 對每對 cluster $(A, B)$，算「cluster 間連通度」： $\text{conn}(A, B) = \sum_{i \in A, j \in B} W_{ij} / (|A| \cdot |B|)$， 其中 $W$ 是 KNN connectivity matrix For each cluster pair $(A, B)$, compute inter-cluster connectivity: $\text{conn}(A, B) = \sum_{i \in A, j \in B} W_{ij} / (|A| \cdot |B|)$ where $W$ is KNN connectivity matrix
  3. 畫 graph：node = cluster、edge = connectivity Plot graph: nodes = clusters, edges = connectivity
  4. 門檻過濾弱邊 → 得到精簡的 cluster topology Threshold weak edges → clean cluster topology
