HVG selection：從 mean-variance 到 LOESS

HVG selection: from mean-variance to LOESS

Normalize 完後，你手上還是 20,000 個基因。但其中絕大多數對辨別細胞型態沒有貢獻—— 它們在所有細胞間都表現得差不多（housekeeping genes、或根本不表現）。 只有變異最大的那 1000-3000 個基因，才會真正讓 PCA 找到「細胞型態之間的差異方向」。

這節用互動散佈圖讓你「親眼選出 HVG」—— 從 mean-variance 圖開始，理解 dispersion / LOESS 為什麼必要、Seurat 跟 Scanpy 怎麼選 top 2000。
After normalize, you still have 20,000 genes. Most contribute nothing to cell-type discrimination — they look the same across all cells (housekeeping, or simply unexpressed). Only the 1000–3000 most variable genes carry the directions PCA cares about.

This section lets you pick HVGs by eye on an interactive mean-variance plot — understanding dispersion, LOESS, and how Seurat vs Scanpy pick top 2000.

🧬 scRNA-seq 視角：HVG 是「警察筆錄裡留下哪些證人」 🧬 scRNA-seq perspective: HVG is "which witnesses make it onto the police report"

你是辦案警察，要從 30,000 個目擊者裡找出兇手線索。但 30,000 個全寫進筆錄不切實際，而且大多數人說的都是「那天我吃了早餐、上班、睡覺」——對破案毫無幫助。你要挑出說法跟別人不一樣的那 2,000 個關鍵證人。scRNA 的HVG selection 就是這個過程：從 30k gene 裡挑出那些「在不同細胞間表現量差異很大」的——它們才帶細胞型態的資訊。 You're a detective sifting 30,000 witnesses for clues. Putting all 30,000 into the report is impractical, and most people just say "I had breakfast, went to work, slept" — useless for the case. You want the 2,000 witnesses whose stories differ wildly. HVG selection IS this process in scRNA: from 30k genes, pick the ones with high cell-to-cell variation — those carry the cell-type information.

問題是：挑錯證人，整個案子破不了。你漏掉 IRF7 這個 pDC 的關鍵 marker，下游所有方法絕對看不到 pDC 這個 cluster——它在 PCA、UMAP、Leiden 都會被「隱形」。這也是為什麼 rare cell（罕見細胞型）的研究對 HVG 策略特別敏感：scanpy 預設 seurat_v3 對 PBMC 大致夠用，但要找 pDC（佔 0.5%）你必須用 Pearson residual HVG（Townes 2019），否則它根本不會出現在你的 figure 上。 The problem: pick the wrong witnesses, the case dies. Miss IRF7 — pDC's key marker — and every downstream method becomes blind to the pDC cluster; PCA, UMAP, Leiden all "lose sight" of it. That's why rare-cell studies are especially HVG-sensitive: scanpy's default seurat_v3 is fine for PBMC overall, but to find pDC (0.5%) you must use Pearson residual HVG (Townes 2019) — otherwise it never makes it into your figure.

⚠️ PCA 之後所有方法只看你挑的那 2000 個 gene——HVG 漏掉某個 marker，整條分析鏈下游絕對看不到那種 cell type。HVG 是單一最重要的「Yes/No 決定」。 ⚠️ Everything after PCA only sees the 2000 HVGs you picked — miss a marker in HVG, downstream cannot see that cell type, period. HVG is the single most consequential yes/no decision.

For PBMC main types, seurat_v3 + 2000 suffices; but the moment you hunt rare cells (pDC, ILC3, stem-like cancer cells), Pearson residual HVG isn't optional — it's mandatory.

§1 mean-variance 關係：你看到什麼？Mean-variance relationship

把每個基因的「平均表現」當 x 軸、「變異」當 y 軸，畫散佈圖。你會看到一條固定形狀的曲線： 低表現 → 變異也低；中表現 → 變異跟著爬升；高表現 → 變異最高。這背後就是 Poisson 或 NB 變異的「mean-variance trend」。

Plot each gene's mean (x) vs variance (y). You see a characteristic curve: low mean → low variance; mid → rising; high → high. This is the Poisson/NB mean-variance trend.

$$\text{Var}(X_j) = \mu_j + \frac{\mu_j^2}{\theta_j} \quad \text{(NB model)}$$
$\mu_j, \theta_j$ 是 gene $j$ 的 NB 參數
$\mu_j, \theta_j$ are NB params for gene $j$

如果所有基因都遵循這條曲線，那就沒有 HVG——所有基因的變異都「正好」對應它的 mean。

If every gene followed this curve exactly, there'd be no HVG — every gene's variance would match its mean.

真正的 HVG 是「變異比曲線預期還高」的基因—— 這代表它在某些細胞特別高、其他細胞特別低——也就是「帶資訊」。

True HVGs have variance above the curve — meaning they spike in some cells, drop in others — i.e., they're informative.

§3 Dispersion 的定義Defining dispersion

最簡單的 HVG 量化指標叫 dispersion：

The simplest HVG metric is dispersion:

$$\text{dispersion}_j = \frac{\text{Var}(x_j)}{\text{Mean}(x_j)}$$
用 mean 去 normalize variance，消除「高 mean 自然 variance 大」的影響
normalize variance by mean to remove "high mean → high variance" effect

在 Poisson 下，所有基因的 dispersion 應該 ≈ 1。HVG 就是「dispersion 顯著大於 1」的。

Under Poisson, all genes have dispersion ≈ 1. HVGs are those with dispersion significantly > 1.

3.1 Dispersion 的不足

3.1 Why dispersion isn't enough

問題是 scRNA-seq 不是純 Poisson 而是 NB——高 mean 基因的 dispersion 本來就會 > 1（overdispersion）。 所以「dispersion > 1」會被高表現基因壟斷，挑出來的 HVG 通通是高表現的。我們需要更聰明的方法。

But scRNA-seq is NB, not Poisson — high-mean genes naturally have dispersion > 1 (overdispersion). "dispersion > 1" gets hijacked by high-mean genes. We need something smarter.

§4 LOESS：把曲線當基準LOESS: use the curve as baseline

LOESS（Locally Estimated Scatterplot Smoothing）是一種局部加權回歸。 在 mean-variance 圖上，它對每個 $\mu$ 周圍的點做局部加權平均，畫出一條平滑曲線當「預期 variance」。 HVG 就是「實測 variance > LOESS 曲線 + k·σ」的基因。

LOESS (Locally Estimated Scatterplot Smoothing) is a local weighted regression. For each $\mu$ on the mean-variance plot, it fits a local weighted average → a smooth curve = "expected variance". HVG = "observed variance > LOESS curve + k·σ".

4.1 LOESS 的核心構造

4.1 LOESS in detail

對每個目標點 $x_0$，做下面三步：

For each target $x_0$, do three steps:

  1. 挑「鄰居」：Pick "neighbors": 所有 $|x_i - x_0| \le h$ 的點
  2. 給權重：Weight: $w_i = (1 - (|x_i - x_0|/h)^3)^3$ (tricube kernel)
  3. 加權回歸：Weighted regression: 對 $\{(x_i, y_i, w_i)\}$ 做 weighted linear / quadratic regression，取 $x_0$ 處的預測值
為什麼用 LOESS 而非全域回歸? Why LOESS, not global regression? mean-variance 關係不是直線——它在低 mean 跟高 mean 處的斜率不一樣。全域回歸會「平均掉」這個差異；LOESS 對每個 $\mu$ 估計局部斜率，更貼近真實趨勢。 The mean-variance relationship isn't linear — slope differs at low vs high mean. Global regression "averages out" the difference; LOESS fits local slopes, tracking the true trend.

4.2 互動：bandwidth h 的影響

4.2 Interactive: effect of bandwidth h

拉滑桿改變 bandwidth $h$（鄰居範圍）：太小 → 曲線抖動、太大 → 變直線。

Drag bandwidth $h$: too small → wobbly, too large → straight line.

實務上 Scanpy 預設 $h \approx 0.3$（在 log mean-variance 空間）。

In practice, Scanpy defaults to $h \approx 0.3$ on log mean-variance.

5.1 Seurat vst 的詳細步驟

5.1 Seurat vst step-by-step

  1. 對每個 gene 算 mean 跟 varianceCompute mean and variance per gene
  2. 在 log 空間做 LOESS：LOESS in log space: $\widehat{\log \sigma^2}(g) = \text{LOESS}(\log \bar{x}_g)$
  3. 每個基因的 expected variance：Expected variance per gene: $\hat{\sigma}_g^2 = \exp(\widehat{\log \sigma^2}(g))$
  4. z-score 標準化每個 cell 的表現：Z-score standardize per cell: $z_{ij} = \min\!\left(\sqrt{n}, \frac{x_{ij} - \bar{x}_j}{\hat{\sigma}_j}\right)$（用 $\sqrt{n}$ clip 防止離群值主導）
  5. 每個基因取 z 的 variance 當 「standardized variance」，取前 N。Variance of z per gene = "standardized variance"; pick top N.

§7 HVG → PCA → clustering 的因果鏈HVG → PCA → clustering causal chain

為什麼挑 HVG 直接決定後續分群結果？

Why does HVG selection directly determine clustering?

  1. HVG 餵 PCA：只用 2000 個 HVG，所以 PCA 找的「最大變異方向」就是「HVG 中變異最大」的線性組合。 HVG feeds PCA: only 2000 HVGs in, so PCA's "max variance direction" is a linear combo of those 2000.
  2. PCA 餵 KNN graph：用 PC1-50 算細胞間 cosine 距離。距離 < 1/k 的成為鄰居。 PCA feeds KNN: PC1-50 → cosine distance → top-k neighbors per cell.
  3. KNN 餵 Leiden/Louvain：在這張圖上做模組度最大化，得到 clusters。 KNN feeds Leiden/Louvain: maximize modularity on the graph → clusters.
後果Implication: 如果你的 HVG 漏掉一個重要 marker（例如 CD3D），下游 PCA → KNN → clustering 都會看不到那個方向，T 細胞 vs B 細胞可能分不開。 If your HVG misses a key marker (e.g., CD3D), downstream PCA → KNN → clustering all lose that direction. T vs B may not separate.

§11 scTransform v2 + analytic Pearson HVG：低表現 marker 不被埋沒scTransform v2 + analytic Pearson HVG: low-expr markers preserved

經典 HVG 方法（seurat_v3 等）有一個盲點：低表現但生物上重要的 marker（如 IL3、IL21）會因為 variance 跟低 mean 一樣小而被埋沒。 Choudhary & Satija (2022) sctransform v2 + Lause 2021 analytic Pearson 改進這個。

Classical HVG (seurat_v3 etc.) has a blind spot: low-expr but biologically important markers (e.g., IL3, IL21) get buried because their variance matches low-mean baseline. Choudhary & Satija (2022) sctransform v2 + Lause 2021 analytic Pearson improve this.

11.1 用 Pearson residual variance 排序

11.1 Rank by Pearson residual variance

對每基因算 Pearson residual 然後算 variance：

Compute Pearson residuals per gene, then variance:

$$z_{ij} = \frac{x_{ij} - \hat{\mu}_{ij}}{\sqrt{\hat{\mu}_{ij} + \hat{\mu}_{ij}^2/\theta}}, \quad \text{HVG score}_j = \text{Var}_i(z_{ij})$$
不依賴 mean 的 variance — 高低表現 gene 公平比較
mean-independent variance — fair comparison across expression levels

效果：所有 gene 的 Pearson residual 在 NB 假設下 variance ≈ 1。HVG 就是「variance >> 1」的——絕對閾值，不受 mean 干擾。

Effect: under NB assumption all genes' Pearson residuals have variance ≈ 1. HVG = "variance >> 1" — absolute threshold, mean-independent.

11.2 "New" markers Pearson HVG finds

§12 多 batch HVG 的兩難：先 HVG 還是先 batch correct？Multi-batch HVG dilemma: HVG-first or batch-correct-first?

你有 5 個病人 × 3 個時間點 = 15 個 samples。HVG 該怎麼挑？ 選項 A：合併所有 sample，挑全域 HVG。問題：被 batch effect 主導（不同 sample 之間表現差異 ≠ 細胞型態差異）。 選項 B：先 Harmony / scVI 校正，再挑 HVG。問題：校正本身需要 HVG 當輸入，雞生蛋。

You have 5 patients × 3 timepoints = 15 samples. How to pick HVG? Option A: pool all samples, pick global HVG. Problem: dominated by batch effects (sample-to-sample diff ≠ cell-type diff). Option B: Harmony / scVI first, then HVG. Problem: correction itself needs HVG as input — chicken and egg.

12.1 解法：batch-aware HVG (Stuart 2019)

12.1 Solution: batch-aware HVG (Stuart 2019)

scanpy 用 batch_key：

scanpy supports batch_key:

sc.pp.highly_variable_genes(
    adata, n_top_genes=2000,
    batch_key="sample",    # 對每 batch 各別算 HVG，取 intersection
    flavor="seurat_v3"
)
# 內部：在每 sample 跑 HVG → 算「該 gene 被多少 sample 選為 HV」
# 排序：先按「被多少 sample 選」、再按 median rank
為何這樣有效Why this works: 「在多個 batch 都被認為 HV」的 gene 才是真實生物訊號 — 跨 batch 一致說明不是 batch artifact。 「只在某 batch HV」的可能是該 batch 特定 artifact（PCR contamination、wash differences）。 Genes flagged HV in multiple batches represent real biology — consistency across batches means not a batch artifact. Genes HV in only one batch may be batch-specific artifacts (PCR contamination, washing differences).
