Scaling：z-score 與 regress-out

Scaling: z-score and regress-out

PCA 在尋找「最大變異方向」時，絕對量級大的基因會壟斷答案。 一個高表現但變異一般的基因，原本 σ ≈ 5；另一個重要 marker 但變異稍微小一點，σ ≈ 1。 PCA 看絕對 σ，第一個會被當主軸——但你真正想要的方向其實在第二個。

Scaling = z-score 把所有 HVG 拉成「σ = 1」，PCA 才能公平比較。 這節用幾何互動圖解釋：先中心化（拉到原點）、再除以 σ。 最後談 regress-out 怎麼從輸入中移除已知干擾（總 counts、MT%）。
PCA finds "maximum variance direction" — but genes with larger absolute magnitudes hijack the answer. A highly-expressed but moderately-variable gene might have σ ≈ 5; an important marker with slightly smaller σ ≈ 1. PCA picks the first as principal axis — but you actually want the second.

Scaling = z-score brings all HVGs to σ = 1, so PCA can compare fairly. This section explains the geometry: first center (pull to origin), then divide by σ. Finally, regress-out removes known nuisances (total counts, MT%) from input.

🧬 scRNA-seq 視角：scaling 是「強迫每個基因發言時間一樣長」 🧬 scRNA-seq perspective: scaling forces every gene to "speak the same length of time"

想像一場「細胞型態圓桌會議」，每個 gene 都是一位發言人。PCA 是會議紀錄員，它會把「發言最多的人」的觀點寫進 PC1。問題來了：MALAT1 是個愛講話的大叔（每個細胞都喊著「我在！我在！」），他講話的音量（變異數）是 CD3D 的 9 倍。會議紀錄員把整個 PC1 都寫成 MALAT1 的廢話，CD3D 講的「我是 T 細胞 marker」反而沒人聽到。 Picture a cell-type round-table meeting where every gene is a speaker. PCA is the secretary, writing whoever talks most into PC1. Problem: MALAT1 is the chatty uncle ("I'm here! I'm here!" in every cell), and his volume (variance) is 9× CD3D's. The secretary fills PC1 entirely with MALAT1 babble, and CD3D's important "I'm a T-cell marker" gets drowned out.

Scaling 就是會議主持人說：「請每個發言人都只能講 1 分鐘，不准超時」（z-score 把每個 gene 拉成 σ=1）。瞬間 MALAT1 的音量被壓下來，CD3D 跟所有 marker gene 公平競爭——PCA 終於能寫出「誰是 B 細胞、誰是 T 細胞」這種有意義的紀錄。順便還有clip @ 10規則，防止某個發言人突然大喊一聲（outlier）把整個會議帶歪。 Scaling is the moderator's rule: "each speaker gets exactly 1 minute, no overtime" (z-score forces every gene to σ=1). Suddenly MALAT1's volume drops, CD3D competes on equal terms — PCA can finally write meaningful minutes like "this is B cells, that's T cells". And a clip @ 10 rule prevents one speaker from screaming once (outlier) and derailing the whole meeting.

This is the step that upgrades PCA from a noise meter into a cell-type radar.

§0 故事：兩個基因的量級不同會發生什麼Story: what if two genes have different scales

想像 2D 空間：每個細胞由 2 個基因表達定位， Gene1（高表現家管基因，σ=5）跟 Gene2（細胞型態 marker，σ=1）。 你會發現點雲是個扁長的橢圓——水平方向長 5 倍。

Imagine 2D space: each cell positioned by 2 genes, Gene1 (high-expression housekeeping, σ=5) and Gene2 (cell-type marker, σ=1). The point cloud is a stretched ellipse — 5× longer horizontally.

§0-1 不 scaling 的 PCA

§0-1 PCA without scaling

PC1 找到的方向會是「橢圓的長軸」——也就是水平方向（Gene1 主導）。 可是 Gene1 是 housekeeping、根本不分細胞型態；真正帶資訊的 Gene2 卻被忽略。

PC1 picks the "ellipse's major axis" = horizontal (Gene1-dominated). But Gene1 is housekeeping — useless for cell types. Informative Gene2 is ignored.

§0-2 Scaling 後的 PCA

§0-2 PCA after scaling

把兩個基因都拉成 σ=1 後，點雲變成近似圓形，PCA 看到的「最大變異」變成真正的細胞型態方向。

After scaling both to σ=1, the cloud becomes roughly circular; PCA's max variance is now the true cell-type direction.

第一個 ahaFirst aha: PCA 不能直接看「原本的 σ」，因為 σ 大不代表有資訊。Scaling 強制讓所有 gene 在 PCA 眼裡是「平等的」，PCA 才會找結構而不是尺度。 PCA can't take "raw σ" at face value — large σ ≠ informative. Scaling forces equality, so PCA finds structure, not scale.

§1 中心化的幾何意義Geometry of centering

Scaling 分兩步：中心化（centering）和 unit variance。我們先看中心化。

Scaling has two steps: centering and unit variance. First centering.

1.1 數學上

1.1 Math

$$x'_{ij} = x_{ij} - \bar{x}_j, \quad \text{where } \bar{x}_j = \frac{1}{n} \sum_i x_{ij}$$
每個基因減掉它自己的 cell-mean
subtract each gene's own cell-mean

1.2 為什麼 PCA 需要中心化?

1.2 Why does PCA need centering?

PCA 的核心是「共變異數矩陣」$C = \frac{1}{n-1} X^T X$， 這個公式只有在中心化（mean = 0）後才正確計算到變異。 如果不中心化、直接做 SVD/eigen，第一個 PC 會指向資料的「整體平均位置」，而非變異方向。

PCA's core is the covariance matrix $C = \frac{1}{n-1} X^T X$, valid only after centering (mean = 0). Without centering, the first PC points to "overall mean position" rather than variance direction.

注意Note: 中心化 ≠ scaling。中心化只是「拉到原點」；scaling 還包含「除以 σ」。Scanpy sc.pp.scale 預設兩步都做。 centering ≠ scaling. Centering only "pulls to origin"; scaling also "divides by σ". Scanpy's sc.pp.scale defaults to both.

§2 z-score 的數學z-score math

$$z_{ij} = \frac{x_{ij} - \bar{x}_j}{\sigma_j}, \quad \sigma_j = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n-1} \sum_i (x_{ij} - \bar{x}_j)^2}$$
每個基因 z-score 標準化：mean = 0，σ = 1
z-score per gene: mean = 0, σ = 1

2.1 性質

2.1 Properties

  • 每個 gene 都變成 mean 0、σ 1。Each gene now has mean 0, σ 1.
  • 細胞間「比較」變成「比較 z 值」——z = 2 表示「比此基因平均高 2σ」。Cell comparison = comparing z-values. z = 2 means "2σ above this gene's mean".
  • 所有基因現在單位一致，PCA 公平比較。All genes share the same unit — fair PCA.

2.2 為什麼還要 clip?

2.2 Why also clip?

Scanpy 預設 sc.pp.scale(adata, max_value=10)——意思是把 z > 10 的「離群細胞-基因組合」clip 在 10。 為什麼？因為 scRNA-seq 偶爾會出現「極端高」的 outlier（單一細胞單一基因 count = 1000），z 可能高達 50+，這會讓 PCA 被這一個點主導。

Scanpy defaults sc.pp.scale(adata, max_value=10) — clip z > 10 at 10. Why? scRNA-seq occasionally has extreme outliers (single cell + gene count = 1000), z can hit 50+, hijacking PCA.

§4 為什麼要 unit varianceWhy unit variance

中心化是為了讓「PCA 的數學公式正確」；unit variance（除以 σ）是為了讓「不同單位的特徵公平比較」。

Centering is for "correct PCA math"; unit variance is for "fair comparison across different-scale features".

4.1 一個極端例子

4.1 An extreme example

假設你的 HVG 包含 GAPDH（高表現家管，count 平均 100）和 CD3D（marker，count 平均 5）。 log normalize 後：GAPDH 變異 σ ≈ 1.5，CD3D 變異 σ ≈ 0.5。 不 scale 時，PCA 會把 GAPDH 當主軸（σ 大 3 倍 → 變異大 9 倍）；scale 後，CD3D 跟 GAPDH 在 PCA 眼裡同等重要，PCA 開始找「細胞型態之間的差異」。

Suppose HVGs include GAPDH (housekeeping, mean count 100) and CD3D (marker, mean count 5). After log normalize: GAPDH σ ≈ 1.5, CD3D σ ≈ 0.5. Without scaling, PCA prioritizes GAPDH (σ 3× → var 9×); after scaling, they're equally weighted, PCA finds cell-type structure.

§5 regress-out：移除已知干擾regress-out: remove known confounders

即使做了 QC + normalization，每個細胞還是有「殘留的 total_counts 差異」、「MT% 差異」、「cell cycle 階段」這些「已知的干擾變數」。 Regress-out 對每個 gene 做迴歸：

Even after QC + normalize, each cell still has "residual total_counts variation", "MT% variation", "cell cycle phase" — known confounders. Regress-out fits per-gene regression:

$$x_{ij} = \alpha_j + \beta_j \cdot c_i + \varepsilon_{ij}, \quad \text{output: } \varepsilon_{ij} = x_{ij} - \alpha_j - \beta_j \cdot c_i$$
$c_i$ 是 confounder（如 total_counts），output 就是 residual
$c_i$ is the confounder (e.g., total_counts), output is the residual

§6 與 ComBat / Harmony 的對應Connection to ComBat / Harmony

Regress-out 處理「每細胞的連續 confounder」（如 total_counts）。ComBat / Harmony 處理「批次效應」（離散的批次標籤）。三者本質是「從 PCA 看到的方向中移除已知雜訊」這個任務的不同變形：

Regress-out handles per-cell continuous confounders (e.g., total_counts). ComBat/Harmony handle batch effects (discrete batch labels). All three solve "remove known noise from PCA directions" in different forms:

§9 深入：什麼情況可以跳過 scaling？— Pearson residual / GLM-PCA 的數學保證Deep dive: when to skip scaling? — Pearson residual / GLM-PCA math guarantee

Heumos 2023 建議「大資料用 Pearson residual 可以省 scale」。為什麼？因為 Pearson residual 在 NB 假設下自帶 σ=1，不需再 scale。

Heumos 2023 recommends "skip scale on large data when using Pearson residual". Why? Because Pearson residual self-normalizes to σ=1 under NB assumption, no extra scale needed.

9.1 數學證明

9.1 Math proof

對 NB$(\mu, \theta)$ 分布的隨機變數 $X$：

For NB$(\mu, \theta)$ random variable $X$:

$$z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sqrt{\mu + \mu^2/\theta}}, \quad E[z] = 0, \quad \text{Var}(z) = \frac{\text{Var}(X)}{\mu + \mu^2/\theta} = 1$$

所以對任何 gene $j$，無論其 expression 高低，Pearson residual 都有 mean 0、variance 1。直接餵 PCA 不需 sc.pp.scale。

So for any gene $j$, regardless of expression level, Pearson residual has mean 0, variance 1. Feed PCA directly — no sc.pp.scale needed.

9.2 跳過 scale 的好處

9.2 Benefits of skipping scale

  • 保持 sparsity：Pearson residual 之後資料還是 sparse（多數 cell 多數 gene z ≈ 0）；sc.pp.scale 會 densify
  • Preserves sparsity: Pearson residuals stay sparse (most cell-gene z ≈ 0); sc.pp.scale densifies
  • 記憶體：1M cells × 2000 HVG sparse 才 ~100 MB；dense 16 GB
  • Memory: 1M cells × 2000 HVG sparse is ~100 MB; dense 16 GB
  • 速度：sparse PCA 比 dense PCA 快 10×（Lanczos 上 SpMV）
  • Speed: sparse PCA is 10× faster than dense (SpMV in Lanczos)

§10 完整 regress-out 推導：OLS 跟 generalized least squaresComplete regress-out derivation: OLS and generalized least squares

上節談過 regress-out 概念。本節給完整數學推導：對每 gene 跑 OLS 是什麼意思？什麼時候要用 GLS？

Earlier we covered the concept. Here's the full math: what does per-gene OLS actually solve? When do we need GLS?

10.1 OLS 推導

10.1 OLS derivation

對 gene $j$，建模：

For gene $j$, model:

$$x_j = \mathbf{C} \boldsymbol{\beta}_j + \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_j, \quad \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_j \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_j^2 I)$$
$\mathbf{C} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times k}$ 是 confounder 矩陣（total_counts, MT% 等）
$\mathbf{C} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times k}$ is confounder matrix (total_counts, MT% etc.)
$$\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_j = (\mathbf{C}^T \mathbf{C})^{-1} \mathbf{C}^T x_j, \quad \text{residual: } r_j = x_j - \mathbf{C} \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_j$$

把 $r_j$ 當「校正後 expression」進 PCA。整個矩陣的 closed-form：

Use $r_j$ as "corrected expression" for PCA. Matrix closed-form:

$$X_{\text{corrected}} = X - \mathbf{C}(\mathbf{C}^T\mathbf{C})^{-1}\mathbf{C}^T X = (I - P_{\mathbf{C}}) X$$
$P_{\mathbf{C}}$ 是投影到 $\mathbf{C}$ 列空間，$I - P_{\mathbf{C}}$ 是投影到其正交補
$P_{\mathbf{C}}$ projects onto $\mathbf{C}$'s column space; $I - P_{\mathbf{C}}$ is its orthogonal complement

10.2 何時 OLS 失敗 → 改用 GLS

10.2 When OLS fails → use GLS

OLS 假設 $\varepsilon$ 變異跨細胞同 σ。但 scRNA 不是——高表現 cell 變異更大（NB variance）。改用 generalized least squares (GLS)：

OLS assumes constant $\varepsilon$ variance across cells. scRNA isn't — high-expression cells have larger variance (NB). Use generalized least squares (GLS):

$$\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_j^{\text{GLS}} = (\mathbf{C}^T W \mathbf{C})^{-1} \mathbf{C}^T W x_j, \quad W = \text{diag}(1 / \text{Var}_i(x_j))$$

scanpy sc.pp.regress_out 預設用 OLS（簡單快）。對需要更精確的應用，可以用 statsmodels.NegativeBinomial 跑 NB GLM。

scanpy's sc.pp.regress_out uses OLS (simple, fast). For precise applications, use statsmodels.NegativeBinomial for NB GLM.

regress-out 的陷阱Regress-out pitfall: 如果 confounder 跟生物 signal 相關，regress-out 會把真實生物變異也吸走。例如 cell cycle 跟 cancer cell proliferation 高度相關，regress out cell cycle 可能讓 cancer cluster 消失。分析 cell cycle 相關問題時不要 regress out。 If confounder correlates with biology, regress-out strips real biology too. E.g., cell cycle correlates with cancer proliferation; regressing out cell cycle may erase the cancer cluster. Don't regress out when analyzing cell-cycle-related questions.

📚 References: scanpy sc.pp.regress_out derives from Seurat's ScaleData (Stuart 2019); Buettner et al. (2015) Nat Biotechnol 33:155 (cell cycle correction pioneer).

§11 與 Harmony / scVI 的橋樑：scaling 不是終點，是 batch correction 的起點Bridge to Harmony / scVI: scaling isn't the end, it's batch correction's start

scaling 完成後，PCA 找到「生物學方向」。但如果你的資料是多 sample / 多 batch， PC1-PC10 還是可能被 batch effect 主導——這就是 M6.1 Integration 的場景。

After scaling, PCA finds "biological directions". But for multi-sample / multi-batch data, PC1-PC10 may still be dominated by batch — that's the topic of M6.1 Integration.
