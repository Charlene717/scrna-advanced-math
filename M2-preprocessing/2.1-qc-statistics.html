QC 統計：MAD 離群偵測與 Doublet 模擬

QC Statistics: MAD Outlier Detection and Doublet Simulation

你拿到 10x 的原始輸出。10,000 個細胞，20,000 個基因。 第一個問題：哪些「細胞」其實只是空 droplet？哪些已經死了？哪些是兩顆黏在一起的 doublet？

最直覺的答案：用「平均值 ± 2 個標準差」當門檻。 這節我們會用互動圖給你看為什麼這個直覺會失敗—— 壞細胞本身會把平均跟標準差拉走， 導致你越用越鬆。

然後我們會逐步推出 MAD（中位數絕對偏差）、$1.4826 \times \text{MAD}$ 為什麼是 σ 的無偏估計、 為什麼 QC 要 log 轉換、多變數聯合 QC，以及 Scrublet 怎麼用模擬隨機產生 doublet 來算 doublet score。
You receive raw 10x output: 10,000 cells × 20,000 genes. First question: which "cells" are actually empty droplets? Which are dying? Which are doublets (two cells fused)?

The intuitive answer: threshold at mean ± 2σ. This section shows you interactively why that intuition fails — bad cells pull mean and σ themselves, making the threshold paradoxically looser.

We then derive MAD (Median Absolute Deviation), prove why $1.4826 \times \text{MAD}$ is an unbiased estimator of σ, show why QC needs log-transform, build multi-variate joint QC, and walk through Scrublet — how it simulates random doublets to score each cell.

§0-2 為什麼書上都寫「mean ± 2σ」?

§0-2 Why do textbooks say "mean ± 2σ"?

因為對「真正的高斯分布」而言，mean ± 2σ 蓋住 95.4% 的資料，乾淨利落。 問題是：scRNA-seq 的計數資料不是高斯——它是右偏的，而且我們要找的就是離群值， 而離群值本身會破壞 σ 的估計。這是個邏輯上的循環悖論。

Because for a true Gaussian, mean ± 2σ covers 95.4% — clean and simple. But scRNA-seq counts aren't Gaussian — they're right-skewed, and the outliers we want to find are the ones that corrupt the σ estimate. It's a circular bind.

▸ 一個 5 點的數字例子 ▸ A worked 5-point example

n_genes = {800, 900, 1000, 1100, 100} (一個壞細胞)
mean = 780, σ ≈ 405. mean − 2σ = −30，所以「100」這個壞細胞不會被砍。
median = 900, MAD = median(|800-900|, |900-900|, |1000-900|, |1100-900|, |100-900|) = median(100, 0, 100, 200, 800) = 100.
median − 3·MAD = 600，「100」被正確標為離群。

§1 為什麼平均會被「壞細胞」綁架Why mean is hijacked by bad cells

§0 的互動已經讓你看到結論。這節我們用一個叫 breakdown point（崩潰點）的概念把它說清楚。

The §0 interaction already showed the conclusion. This section introduces breakdown point to make it precise.

1.1 崩潰點 (Breakdown Point) 的定義

1.1 Definition of Breakdown Point

一個統計量 $T(\mathbf{x})$ 的崩潰點是：在污染資料集裡， 「最多可以替換多少比例的觀察值」，使得 $T$ 仍然有限。

The breakdown point of a statistic $T(\mathbf{x})$ is: the maximum fraction of observations that can be replaced (with arbitrarily bad values) before $T$ becomes infinite.

統計量Statistic 崩潰點Breakdown 說明Note
mean $\bar{x}$mean $\bar{x}$ 1/n1/n 只要 1 個樣本變成 ∞，平均就變 ∞ A single ∞ sample makes mean ∞
σσ 1/n1/n 同上，且更敏感（平方） Same, more sensitive (squared)
medianmedian 50%50% 必須過半樣本變壞才會崩潰 More than half must be corrupted to break
MADMAD 50%50% 同 median，因為它就是 |xᵢ−median| 的 median Same as median (it's the median of deviations)
直白翻譯In plain terms: 平均「一個壞蘋果就毀整籃」。中位數「過半才毀」。50% 是上限—— 邏輯上你不可能更穩健，因為過半壞掉的話就根本沒有「正確答案」可言。 Mean: "one bad apple ruins the barrel." Median: "needs half to ruin it." 50% is the theoretical max — beyond it, "correct" loses meaning.

1.2 一個更深的例子：scRNA-seq 的「死細胞群」

1.2 A deeper example: the "dying cell cluster"

scRNA-seq 常有 5%-15% 的細胞屬於壞品質。如果你拿全部一起算 mean / σ， 這 10% 左右的壞細胞會把 σ 拉大 2-3 倍。 你以為設了 mean − 2σ 是「砍尾巴」，實際上 σ 已經先被汙染了，門檻變得太寬。

scRNA-seq routinely has 5%–15% bad cells. Computing mean/σ over everything, those 10% can inflate σ by 2–3×. You think "mean − 2σ" is a tail cutoff, but σ is already contaminated — the cutoff is too lax.

§2 MAD 的定義：中位數的偏差再取中位數MAD definition: median of deviations from median

MAD（Median Absolute Deviation）是一個「兩層 median」的構造。 我們一步一步建構，跟 σ 的「mean → variance → σ」三步流程比對。

MAD (Median Absolute Deviation) is a "two-layer median" construction. We build it step by step, paralleling the "mean → variance → σ" pipeline.

2.1 與標準差 σ 的對比

2.1 Side-by-side with σ

σ 的三步驟

σ in 3 steps

  1. 算中心：Compute center: $\mu = \bar{x} = \frac{1}{n}\sum x_i$
  2. 算每點偏差的平方平均：Mean squared deviation: $\sigma^2 = \frac{1}{n}\sum (x_i - \mu)^2$
  3. 開根號變回原單位：Square-root back to units: $\sigma$

每一步都用「平均」，所以每一步都可被極端值拉走。

Every step uses "mean" — every step is vulnerable.

MAD 的兩步驟

MAD in 2 steps

  1. 算中心：Compute center: $m = \text{median}(x)$
  2. 算每點偏差絕對值的中位數：Median of absolute deviations: $\text{MAD} = \text{median}_i |x_i - m|$

每一步都用「中位數」，所以每一步崩潰點都是 50%。

Every step uses "median" — breakdown point 50% throughout.

$$\boxed{\text{MAD}(\mathbf{x}) \;=\; \text{median}_i\!\left(\,\left|\,x_i - \text{median}_j(x_j)\,\right|\,\right)}$$
MAD = 偏離中位數的絕對值，再取中位數
MAD = median of |sample − median|

2.2 手動算一次：7 個樣本

2.2 Compute by hand: 7 samples

$\mathbf{x} = \{800, 900, 950, 1000, 1050, 1100, 100\}$（其中 100 是壞細胞）

$\mathbf{x} = \{800, 900, 950, 1000, 1050, 1100, 100\}$ (100 is a bad cell)

  1. 排序：Sort: {100, 800, 900, 950, 1000, 1050, 1100} → median = 950
  2. 每點偏差絕對值：Absolute deviations: |100−950|=850, |800−950|=150, |900−950|=50, |950−950|=0, |1000−950|=50, |1050−950|=100, |1100−950|=150
  3. 這 7 個偏差排序：Sort those: {0, 50, 50, 100, 150, 150, 850} → median = 100

所以 MAD = 100。注意「壞細胞」貢獻的偏差 850 在排序後排在最後，被排序自然丟到尾巴， 不影響中間那個 median 值。

So MAD = 100. The bad cell's huge deviation (850) is sorted to the tail — naturally discarded by the median, leaving the central estimate untouched.

關鍵直覺Key intuition: MAD 不是「把離群值忽略」——是「靠排序的結構讓離群值排到中間以外」。 這就是為什麼壞細胞不論多壞都不會影響估計。 MAD doesn't "ignore" outliers — it lets outliers sort themselves to the ends, so the central rank stays clean. Bad cells can't move the median regardless of severity.

§3 1.4826 是哪裡來的？— 從常態分布推導Where does 1.4826 come from? Derivation from normal

常常會看到這行程式：cutoff = median + 1.4826 * MAD * k。 為什麼是 1.4826？這個數字背後是把 MAD「翻譯成」σ 的單位轉換因子。

You often see: cutoff = median + 1.4826 * MAD * k. Why 1.4826? It's the conversion factor that translates MAD into σ-units.

3.1 對高斯資料：MAD 與 σ 的關係

3.1 For Gaussian data: relating MAD and σ

假設 $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$。median = 0，所以 MAD 變成：

Assume $X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$. median = 0, so MAD becomes:

$$\text{MAD} = \text{median}(|X|)$$

由對稱性，$|X|$ 的 median 是「使 $P(|X| \le m) = 0.5$」的 $m$， 也就是 $P(-m \le X \le m) = 0.5$，所以 $P(X \le m) = 0.75$。

By symmetry, the median $m$ of $|X|$ satisfies $P(|X| \le m) = 0.5$, i.e., $P(-m \le X \le m) = 0.5$, hence $P(X \le m) = 0.75$.

$$m = \sigma \cdot \Phi^{-1}(0.75)$$
$\Phi^{-1}$ 是標準常態的累積分布反函數
$\Phi^{-1}$ is the inverse standard-normal CDF

查表（或用 scipy.stats.norm.ppf(0.75)）：

Look up (or scipy.stats.norm.ppf(0.75)):

$$\Phi^{-1}(0.75) \approx 0.67448975 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{MAD} \approx 0.6745\,\sigma$$ $$\boxed{\;\sigma \approx \frac{\text{MAD}}{0.6745} = 1.4826 \times \text{MAD}\;}$$

所以在「真的是高斯」的假設下，1.4826 × MAD 是 σ 的無偏一致估計。 在 QC 程式碼中你會看到這個常數出現，目的是讓 MAD 跟 σ 在同一個單位下比較，k=3 才有「3σ」的物理意義。

So under the Gaussian assumption, 1.4826 × MAD is an unbiased, consistent estimator of σ. This constant appears in QC code so that MAD and σ live in the same units — k=3 then literally means "3σ-equivalent".

3.3 但 scRNA-seq 不是高斯！

3.3 But scRNA-seq isn't Gaussian!

原始的 n_genes、total_counts 都是右偏的計數分布。 直接套 1.4826 是錯誤的——這就是為什麼我們會在 §5 看到「先 log 再算 MAD」。 log 把右偏拉直，讓「高斯假設」近似成立。

Raw n_genes, total_counts are right-skewed count distributions. Applying 1.4826 directly is wrong — that's why §5 introduces "log first, then MAD". Log straightens the skew, making the Gaussian approximation roughly valid.

  • k = 3：類似 3σ，砍掉 ~0.3% 高斯尾巴。適合「資料品質還不錯，想清乾淨」
  • k = 3: ~3σ-equivalent, cuts ~0.3% of a Gaussian tail. Good for "clean data, light filtering"
  • k = 5：較保守，只砍極端壞的細胞。適合「資料量少，怕誤殺」
  • k = 5: conservative, only flags extreme outliers. Good for "small dataset, fear false positives"
  • k 太小：在右偏分布下會誤殺正常的高表現細胞
  • k too small: in right-skewed data, normal high-expressing cells get falsely flagged

§5 為什麼要 log 轉換才算 MADWhy log-transform before computing MAD

total_counts 在 scRNA-seq 裡通常是右偏的——很多細胞 1000-5000，少數細胞 20000-50000。 Histogram 看起來是一條長尾巴拖到右邊。在這種分布上直接算 MAD，「中位數 + k·MAD」這個對稱門檻會在右邊砍掉很多正常的高表現細胞。

total_counts in scRNA-seq is right-skewed — most cells 1000–5000, some 20k–50k. The histogram has a long right tail. Applying MAD directly gives a symmetric cutoff that falsely cuts high-expressing normal cells.

5.2 數學上：log 把對數常態變成常態

5.2 Math: log turns lognormal into normal

$$\text{If } X \sim \text{Lognormal}(\mu, \sigma^2), \text{ then } \log X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2).$$

許多計數分布（包含 scRNA-seq 的 total counts）近似 lognormal——log 後接近 Gaussian，可以用 §3 推導的 1.4826 校正、可以用對稱門檻。 通常用 log10(x+1) 或 log1p(x)，避免 $\log 0$。

Many count distributions (including scRNA-seq total counts) are approximately lognormal — log makes them roughly Gaussian, validating the 1.4826 correction from §3 and the symmetric cutoff. We typically use log10(x+1) or log1p(x) to avoid $\log 0$.

6.3 聯合門檻 vs 單變數門檻

6.3 Joint vs marginal cutoffs

最簡單做法：三個變數各做 1D MAD，「任一變數出界」就砍。這叫邊際 (marginal) 門檻。 缺點：忽略變數間相關性——同時「中等低 nFeature」+「中等高 MT%」其實是死細胞訊號，但任一邊都不到門檻。

Simplest: 1D MAD per variable, cut if any out of range. This is marginal filtering. Limitation: ignores correlations — "moderately low nFeature" + "moderately high MT%" together signal dying cells, but neither individually crosses the threshold.

▸ 進階：Mahalanobis 距離與聯合 robust 門檻 ▸ Advanced: Mahalanobis distance and joint robust cutoffs

聯合方式：用共變異數矩陣的 robust 估計（MCD, Minimum Covariance Determinant）， 算 Mahalanobis 距離 $d_M^2 = (x-\mu)^T \Sigma^{-1} (x-\mu)$， 在 robust 距離大於某門檻時標為離群。MCD 的崩潰點達 50%。 Python：sklearn.covariance.MinCovDet。實務中 scRNA-seq 較少用，因為 marginal 已經夠好且簡單。

Joint approach: use a robust covariance matrix estimator (MCD, Minimum Covariance Determinant). Compute Mahalanobis distance $d_M^2 = (x-\mu)^T \Sigma^{-1} (x-\mu)$, threshold on the robust distance. MCD has 50% breakdown point. Python: sklearn.covariance.MinCovDet. Rarely used in scRNA-seq practice because marginal is good enough and simpler.

§7 Scrublet：模擬合成 doublet 算機率Scrublet: simulate synthetic doublets to score probability

Doublet（兩細胞黏在一起被當成一個）佔 ~1-10%，光看 total_counts 偏高並不夠—— 有些 doublet 跟某種細胞型態本身的 high-count 看起來一樣。 Scrublet 用一個聰明的「合成模擬」想法： 既然真實 doublet 是兩個細胞的計數相加，我就在資料裡隨機抓兩個細胞相加當「合成 doublet」， 訓練 KNN 分類器把每個觀察到的細胞跟這些合成 doublet 比，給出 doublet score。

Doublets (two cells captured together) are ~1–10% of data. High total_counts alone isn't enough — some doublets look identical to genuinely high-counting cell types. Scrublet's clever idea: simulate doublets by randomly adding pairs of observed cells together, then train a KNN classifier to score each cell by similarity to those synthetic doublets.

7.1 演算法 4 步驟

7.1 The 4-step algorithm

  1. 合成 doubletSynthesize doublets: 從觀察細胞 $\{c_1, \ldots, c_n\}$ 中隨機抽兩個 $c_i, c_j$，相加得到合成 doublet $d_{ij} = c_i + c_j$。重複 $2n$ 次。 Pick two random cells $c_i, c_j$ from observed $\{c_1, \ldots, c_n\}$. Form synthetic doublet $d_{ij} = c_i + c_j$. Repeat $2n$ times.
  2. 合併資料降維Merge & reduce: 把觀察 + 合成放在一起，做 normalize + PCA，得到 30 維表示。 Concatenate observed + synthetic, normalize + PCA, get 30-D representation.
  3. KNN 找鄰居KNN neighbors: 對每個觀察細胞，找它的 $k$ 個最近鄰（觀察 + 合成都算）。 For each observed cell, find its $k$ nearest neighbors (across observed + synthetic).
  4. 算 doublet scoreCompute doublet score: $\text{score}_i = \frac{\text{鄰居中合成 doublet 的個數}}{k}$。鄰居越多合成 doublet → 細胞越像 doublet。 $\text{score}_i = \frac{\#\text{synthetic neighbors}}{k}$. More synthetic neighbors → more doublet-like.

7.2 為什麼這個直覺有效？

7.2 Why does this intuition work?

關鍵假設：真實 doublet 的表現模式 ≈ 兩個 singlet 表現量「相加」。 這對 mRNA 計數成立，因為兩個細胞混在一個 droplet，mRNA 直接加總。
Key assumption: real doublets ≈ "sum of two singlets" in expression. This holds for mRNA counts because two cells in one droplet pool their mRNAs additively.
注意：兩個同型細胞的 doublet（homotypic）很難偵測——它的表現跟單個高表現 singlet 幾乎一樣。 Scrublet 主要抓異型(heterotypic) doublet。
Caveat: doublets of two same-type cells (homotypic) are hard to detect — they look like a single high-expressing singlet. Scrublet mainly catches heterotypic doublets.

import scanpy as sc, numpy as np
from scipy.stats import median_abs_deviation

adata = sc.read_10x_h5("filtered_feature_bc_matrix.h5")
adata.var["mt"] = adata.var_names.str.startswith("MT-")
sc.pp.calculate_qc_metrics(adata, qc_vars=["mt"], inplace=True)

# step 1: log transform the QC vars (right-skewed counts)
for v in ["n_genes_by_counts", "total_counts"]:
    adata.obs[v + "_log"] = np.log1p(adata.obs[v])

# step 2: MAD-based outlier on log scale
def is_outlier(M, k=5):
    med = np.median(M); mad = median_abs_deviation(M)
    return (M < med - k*mad) | (M > med + k*mad)

adata.obs["outlier"] = (
    is_outlier(adata.obs["n_genes_by_counts_log"], k=5)
    | is_outlier(adata.obs["total_counts_log"], k=5)
    | is_outlier(adata.obs["pct_counts_mt"], k=3)
)

# step 3: doublet score via Scrublet
sc.external.pp.scrublet(adata)
adata.obs["doublet"] = adata.obs["predicted_doublet"]

# step 4: filter
adata = adata[~adata.obs["outlier"] & ~adata.obs["doublet"]].copy()
print(f"Kept {adata.n_obs} cells")

§11 深入：Ambient RNA 污染與 SoupX / DecontX 校正Deep dive: Ambient RNA contamination and SoupX / DecontX correction

即使通過 QC，每個 cell 的 expression 還包含環境 RNA： 從死/破細胞釋放到 droplet 緩衝液裡的 mRNA，會跟著被測到。 典型 contamination rate 5-30%（Young & Behjati 2020）。本節介紹兩個校正工具。

11.1 SoupX 的數學模型

11.1 SoupX math model

$$X_{ij}^{\text{obs}} = X_{ij}^{\text{true}} + \rho_i \cdot s_i \cdot p_j^{\text{soup}}$$
$\rho_i$ = cell $i$ 的污染率、$s_i$ = total counts、$p_j^{\text{soup}}$ = soup 中 gene $j$ 的比例
$\rho_i$ = cell $i$ contamination, $s_i$ = total counts, $p_j^{\text{soup}}$ = gene $j$ fraction in soup

SoupX 用「空 droplet」估 $p_j^{\text{soup}}$（empty droplet = pure soup），然後用 cell 裡不該表現的 marker（如 T cell 不該有 immunoglobulin）估 $\rho_i$。

SoupX uses empty droplets to estimate $p_j^{\text{soup}}$ (empty = pure soup), and "should-not-be-expressed" markers (e.g., T cells shouldn't have immunoglobulin) to estimate $\rho_i$.
