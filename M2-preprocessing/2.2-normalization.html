Normalization 的數學

The Math of Normalization

log(x+1) 從何而來？SCTransform 的 Pearson residuals 又是什麼？正則化負二項回歸完整推導。

Where does log(x+1) come from? What are SCTransform's Pearson residuals? Full regularized NB regression.

直覺 / Intuition

為什麼要 Normalization？

Why normalize?

同樣是「CD3D 表達 100」，在細胞 A 共捕捉 5,000 分子跟細胞 B 共捕捉 20,000 分子下完全不一樣—— 前者代表「CD3D 是高表現基因」、後者代表「普通基因」。 必須先校正 sequencing depth，才能跨細胞比較表現量。

"CD3D = 100" means different things in cell A (5,000 total UMIs) vs cell B (20,000 total UMIs). Depth correction is necessary before comparing across cells.

核心數學 / Math

① LogNormalize （Seurat 預設）(Seurat default)

$$\tilde{x}_{ij} = \log\left( \frac{x_{ij}}{\sum_k x_{ik}} \cdot 10^4 + 1 \right)$$
3 個操作：除以該細胞總計數、乘 10,000（scale factor）、log(.+1)
Three steps: divide by total count, scale to 10^4, log(.+1)

為什麼 log？表現量呈右偏分布，log 後接近常態，方便後續 PCA。
為什麼 +1？避免 $\log(0) = -\infty$，且把零保留為 $\log 1 = 0$。

Why log? Right-skewed expressions become near-normal after log, suitable for PCA. Why +1? Avoids $\log(0)$ and keeps zeros as 0.

⚠️ LogNormalize 的副作用⚠️ Side effect of LogNormalize

過度校正：low-depth 細胞被「放大」，低表現基因被高估方差，造成下游 PCA 受 sparse genes 主宰。SCTransform 就是要解決這個。

Over-correction: low-depth cells get amplified, low-expression genes have inflated variance, leading PCA dominated by sparse genes. SCTransform addresses this.

② SCTransform （Hafemeister & Satija 2019）

對每個基因擬合一個負二項 GLM：

Fit a Negative Binomial GLM for each gene:

$$\log(\mu_{ij}) = \beta_0^{(j)} + \beta_1^{(j)} \log(n_i), \quad x_{ij} \sim \text{NB}(\mu_{ij}, \theta_j)$$
$n_i$ = 細胞 $i$ 總計數；$\beta_0^{(j)}, \beta_1^{(j)}, \theta_j$ 是基因 $j$ 專屬參數
$n_i$ = total counts of cell $i$; $\beta_0^{(j)}, \beta_1^{(j)}, \theta_j$ are gene-specific

然後用 Pearson residual 當作標準化後的值：

Use Pearson residuals as normalized values:

$$z_{ij} = \frac{x_{ij} - \hat\mu_{ij}}{\sqrt{\hat\mu_{ij} + \hat\mu_{ij}^2 / \hat\theta_j}}$$
分母 = NB 的標準差。「比模型預期高/低多少個 σ」
Denominator = NB std. "How many σ above/below model prediction"

「Regularized」是什麼意思？

What does "regularized" mean?

參數 $\beta_0, \beta_1, \theta$ 對低表現基因會擬得不穩。SCTransform 用核密度回歸把這三個參數作為「平均表現量」的平滑函數，借相鄰基因的資訊使估計更穩定。

Parameters fluctuate wildly for low-expression genes. SCTransform applies kernel regression to smooth $\beta_0, \beta_1, \theta$ as functions of mean expression, borrowing strength across genes.

③ scran （pool-based normalization）

用「細胞池」估計 size factor，再用線性回歸去除單細胞噪音。對稀少 cell type有時比 SCTransform 穩定。

Pool-based estimation of size factors, then linear deconvolution. Sometimes more stable for rare cell types than SCTransform.

程式碼 / Code 
# LogNormalize
sc.pp.normalize_total(adata, target_sum=1e4)
sc.pp.log1p(adata)

# Pearson residuals (Lause et al. 2021 — simplified SCTransform)
sc.experimental.pp.normalize_pearson_residuals(adata, theta=100)

# LogNormalize (Seurat default)
pbmc <- NormalizeData(pbmc, normalization.method = "LogNormalize", scale.factor = 10000)

# SCTransform (推薦做法)
pbmc <- SCTransform(pbmc, vst.flavor = "v2", verbose = FALSE)

# scran (size factor)
library(scran)
sce <- computeSumFactors(sce, clusters = quickCluster(sce))
sce <- logNormCounts(sce)
