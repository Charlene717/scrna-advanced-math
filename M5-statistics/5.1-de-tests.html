M5.1 · STATISTICAL INFERENCE

DE 檢定：Wilcoxon · MAST · DESeq2

DE Tests

三大主流方法的數學差異。為什麼 Wilcoxon 是 Seurat 預設？MAST 的 hurdle model 怎麼處理零？

The math of three mainstream methods. Why Wilcoxon is Seurat default. How MAST's hurdle handles zeros.

直覺 / Intuition

分群之後最常問：「哪些基因在 cluster A 跟 cluster B 之間有差異？」這是 DE 分析的核心問題。三大方法有不同假設與適用情境。

After clustering: which genes differ between cluster A and B? Three mainstream tests with different assumptions.

核心數學 / Math

① Wilcoxon rank-sum test （Seurat 預設）

完全無分布假設。把兩組所有表現量混合排序，然後比較兩組的「排名總和」。

Fully distribution-free. Merge and rank all expressions, compare rank sums.

$$U = R_1 - \frac{n_1(n_1+1)}{2}, \quad Z = \frac{U - n_1 n_2 / 2}{\sqrt{n_1 n_2 (n_1 + n_2 + 1)/12}}$$

優點：穩健、快、不需擔心分布。
缺點：忽略「強度」資訊，只看排名；計數中大量零讓排名平手很多。

Pros: robust, fast, distribution-free. Cons: ignores magnitude; many ties from zeros.

② MAST 的 hurdle 模型

scRNA-seq 計數有兩個成分：「是否表達」(0/非 0)、「表達多少」(正值)。MAST 用兩個模型分別建模：

scRNA-seq counts have two components: expressed (0 vs nonzero) and magnitude. MAST fits two models:

$$\underbrace{P(X_{ij} > 0) = \text{logit}^{-1}(\alpha_0 + \alpha_1 \cdot g_j + \alpha_2 \cdot \text{CDR}_j)}_{\text{discrete: expressed or not}}$$ $$\underbrace{X_{ij} \mid X_{ij} > 0 \sim \mathcal{N}(\beta_0 + \beta_1 g_j + \beta_2 \text{CDR}_j, \sigma^2)}_{\text{continuous: how much}}$$
CDR = cellular detection rate（細胞檢測率），用來控制 cell-level 變異
CDR = cellular detection rate (controls cell-level variation)

兩個 likelihood 相乘做聯合檢定。優點：明確區分「該不該表達」與「表達多少」、能加 covariate。

Joint LRT on combined likelihood. Pros: separates "expressed?" from "how much"; supports covariates.

③ DESeq2 負二項 GLM

$$x_{ij} \sim \text{NB}(\mu_{ij}, \theta_j), \quad \log(\mu_{ij}) = \log(s_i) + \beta_0^{(j)} + \beta_1^{(j)} \cdot g_i$$
$s_i$ = size factor；$\beta_1^{(j)}$ = log fold change；$\theta_j$ 用 empirical Bayes 估計
$s_i$ = size factor; $\beta_1^{(j)}$ = log fold change; $\theta_j$ via empirical Bayes

用 Wald test 檢驗 $H_0: \beta_1^{(j)} = 0$。適合 pseudo-bulk 分析（先把同 cluster 細胞加總），不直接用在 single-cell。

Wald test on $H_0: \beta_1^{(j)} = 0$. Best for pseudo-bulk (sum cells per cluster), not raw single-cell.

⚠️ Pseudo-bulk 是現代共識 ⚠️ Pseudo-bulk is the modern consensus

Squair et al. 2021、Murphy & Skene 2022 都顯示：單獨用 single-cell DE 會嚴重高估 p-value 顯著性，因為它把同一個體內的細胞當作獨立樣本。建議：先做pseudo-bulk（個體層級加總），再用 DESeq2 / edgeR / limma。

Squair et al. (2021), Murphy & Skene (2022): cell-level DE inflates significance by treating cells from one individual as independent. Modern advice: pseudo-bulk first (sum per donor), then DESeq2/edgeR/limma.
