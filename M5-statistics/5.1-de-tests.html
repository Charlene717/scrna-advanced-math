DE tests：找出 cluster 之間的 marker genes

DE tests: finding marker genes between clusters

Leiden 把你的 10,000 個細胞分成 10 群。下一步：每群的「marker genes」是什麼？ 哪些基因在某群表現顯著高（→ 給你細胞型態註解的線索）？這就是 DE (Differential Expression) test。

本節從最簡單的 t-test 開始，理解為什麼scRNA 不該用 t-test（離散、零膨脹、非高斯）、 為什麼 scanpy 預設 Wilcoxon rank-sum、MAST 的雙部分模型怎麼處理零、 以及為什麼pseudobulk + DESeq2在 multi-sample 是金標準。 最後用互動 volcano plot 看 effect size 跟 p-value 怎麼一起決定 marker。
Leiden split your 10k cells into 10 clusters. Next: what are each cluster's marker genes? Which genes are significantly higher in some clusters (→ cell-type annotation clues)? This is DE testing.

We start from t-test, see why scRNA shouldn't use t-test (discrete, zero-inflated, non-Gaussian), why Scanpy defaults to Wilcoxon, MAST's two-part model for zeros, and why pseudobulk + DESeq2 is gold-standard for multi-sample data. Interactive volcano plot to see how effect size and p-value jointly determine markers.

🧬 scRNA-seq 視角：DE 是給 cluster「取名字」的命名儀式 🧬 scRNA-seq perspective: DE is the naming ceremony for your clusters

想像你領養了 10 隻流浪貓，但暫時只能叫牠們「1 號、2 號 … 10 號」。要怎麼幫每隻取名字？看牠們的特徵——「1 號特別愛吃魚（魚控）」、「2 號全身橘色（橘子）」、「3 號每天午夜亂叫（夜半歌后）」。scRNA 的 DE 就是這個取名儀式：Leiden 給你 cluster 0, 1, 2... 一堆數字，DE 找出每個 cluster表現特別高的 marker gene，再讓你對照「CD3D → T cell、CD79A → B cell、CD14 → monocyte」幫每群取名字。 Imagine you've adopted 10 stray cats, temporarily called "#1, #2, … #10". How do you name them? You watch their traits — "#1 loves fish (Fishie)", "#2 is fully orange (Mandarin)", "#3 sings at midnight (Midnight Diva)". scRNA's DE is this naming ceremony: Leiden gives you cluster 0, 1, 2... DE finds the genes each cluster expresses dramatically more than others, and you map "CD3D → T cell, CD79A → B cell, CD14 → monocyte" to assign names.

但 scRNA 的 DE 有三個跟 bulk RNA-seq 完全不一樣的陷阱：(1) 資料 70-90% 是 0（你想像每隻貓只被觀察 10 秒，多數行為都「沒看到」）→ t-test 的「常態假設」整個崩盤，要改用 Wilcoxon 或 MAST。(2) pseudoreplication 陷阱：同 patient 1000 個 cell 不是 1000 個獨立樣本（同一家庭養的 5 隻貓不算 5 個獨立家庭的數據）；要算「疾病 vs 健康」，必須用 pseudobulk——把同 patient 同 cluster 的 cells 合成 1 個 bulk sample 再比（Squair 2021、Crowell 2020）。(3) 30k gene 一次測 → 必須馬上串到 §M5.2 BH 校正，否則1500 個假 marker 會偽裝成發現。 But scRNA DE has three traps absent from bulk RNA-seq: (1) 70-90% of entries are 0 (imagine each cat watched for 10 seconds — most behaviors "not seen") → t-test's normality assumption collapses; use Wilcoxon or MAST. (2) Pseudoreplication trap: 1000 cells from one patient are not 1000 independent samples (5 cats from one household ≠ 5 independent household data points); for "disease vs healthy", use pseudobulk — sum same-cluster cells per patient into one bulk sample first (Squair 2021, Crowell 2020). (3) 30k genes at once → chain immediately into §M5.2 BH correction, or 1500 fake markers pose as discoveries.

§1 t-test 與 Wilcoxon rank-sum 的直覺t-test vs Wilcoxon intuition

1.1 t-test：比較兩群平均

1.1 t-test: compare two means

$$t = \frac{\bar{x}_A - \bar{x}_B}{\sqrt{s_A^2/n_A + s_B^2/n_B}}$$
分子 = 平均差、分母 = 估計的差異標準誤
numerator = mean diff, denominator = estimated SE of diff

假設：兩群資料皆近似常態分布。在 H₀（無差異）下，$t$ 服從 t-分布。

Assumption: both groups approximately normal. Under H₀, $t$ follows t-distribution.

1.2 Wilcoxon rank-sum：比較「排名」而非「數值」

1.2 Wilcoxon rank-sum: ranks, not values

  1. 把 A、B 兩群所有資料合在一起排序Pool A, B and rank all together
  2. 算 A 群 rank sum：A's rank sum: $R_A = \sum_{i \in A} \text{rank}(x_i)$
  3. H₀ 下 $R_A \approx \frac{n_A (n_A+n_B+1)}{2}$Under H₀, $R_A \approx \frac{n_A (n_A+n_B+1)}{2}$
  4. $R_A$ 偏離過大 → 拒絕 H₀Large deviation → reject H₀
關鍵差別Key difference: t-test 用「值」（受極端值與分布形狀影響）；Wilcoxon 用「排名」（對分布形狀 robust）。scRNA counts 是離散、右偏、零膨脹，Wilcoxon 比 t-test 自然得多。 t-test uses values (sensitive to outliers and shape); Wilcoxon uses ranks (shape-robust). scRNA counts are discrete, skewed, zero-inflated — Wilcoxon fits better.

§4 MAST 的雙部分模型MAST's two-part model

MAST（Finak et al. 2015）專門針對 scRNA 設計，把每個 gene 的表現量看成兩個過程：

MAST (Finak et al. 2015) is purpose-built for scRNA. It treats each gene's expression as two processes:

$$\begin{aligned} Z_{ij} &\sim \text{Bernoulli}(\pi_{ij}) \quad &\text{(detected: 0 or 1)} \\ Y_{ij} | Z_{ij} = 1 &\sim \text{Normal}(\mu_{ij}, \sigma^2) \quad &\text{(conditional log-expression)} \end{aligned}$$
$Z$ 模型「偵測到沒」、$Y$ 模型「偵測到時表現量」
$Z$ models "detected or not", $Y$ models "expression given detected"

每部分各做一個 GLM，最後用 likelihood ratio test 合併得單一 p-value。

Each part fits a GLM; combined via likelihood ratio test → single p-value.

4.1 雙部分為何重要

4.1 Why two parts matter

CD3D 在 A 跟 B 的差異可能來自：

CD3D's difference between A and B may come from:

  • 偵測比例不同（A: 90%、B: 30%）→ Z 部分顯著
  • Detection rate differs (A: 90%, B: 30%) → Z significant
  • 偵測到的表現量不同（A: log mean 4.5、B: 2.0）→ Y 部分顯著
  • Conditional expression differs (A: 4.5, B: 2.0) → Y significant

兩個都顯著 → 強 marker。只一個顯著 → 弱 marker。t-test 混在一起反而看不清。

Both significant → strong marker. Only one → weak marker. t-test merges them, losing resolution.

§5 Pseudobulk + DESeq2：金標準Pseudobulk + DESeq2: gold standard

以上都假設「每細胞是獨立樣本」。但實際上每樣本（病人/老鼠）內細胞高度相關。 Squair et al. (2021) 證明：直接在細胞層做 DE 會放大樣本偏誤。正確做法是 pseudobulk。

All above assume "cells are independent". But cells within one sample are highly correlated. Squair et al. (2021) showed: cell-level DE amplifies sample bias. Correct: pseudobulk.

5.1 Pseudobulk 三步驟

5.1 Pseudobulk in 3 steps

  1. 每 (cluster, sample) 把所有 count 加起來，得「假 bulk」樣本 Sum counts across cells per (cluster, sample) → pseudo-bulk
  2. 在 pseudobulks 跑 DESeq2 / edgeR / limma-voom（已驗證的 bulk RNA-seq 方法） Run DESeq2 / edgeR / limma-voom on pseudobulks (validated bulk methods)
  3. 獲得每 gene 的 log2FC + p-value + FDR Get log2FC + p-value + FDR per gene
from pydeseq2.dds import DeseqDataSet
from pydeseq2.ds import DeseqStats

# aggregate cells -> pseudobulks per (sample, cluster)
pb = sc.get.aggregate(adata, by=["sample", "leiden"], func="sum")

dds = DeseqDataSet(counts=pb.X, metadata=pb.obs, design_factors="cluster")
dds.deseq2()
res = DeseqStats(dds, contrast=["cluster", "0", "rest"])
res.summary()
何時用 pseudobulk? When to use pseudobulk? 有 multiple samples（如疾病 vs 健康各 5 人）一定要 pseudobulk。單一樣本內找 cluster marker → Wilcoxon / MAST 即可。 Multiple samples (e.g., 5 disease vs 5 control): pseudobulk required. Within single sample for cluster markers: Wilcoxon / MAST suffices.

§10 深入：MAST hurdle model 完整推導與 likelihood ratio testDeep dive: MAST hurdle model full derivation and likelihood ratio test

之前介紹了 MAST 雙部分模型概念。本節給完整數學：兩個 GLM、likelihood 合併、為何能同時抓「偵測 vs 表現」。

Earlier we introduced MAST concept. Full math: two GLMs, likelihood combination, why it catches "detection vs expression" jointly.

10.1 雙 GLM 公式

10.1 Two GLMs

對 cluster A vs B（用 indicator $g_i$），gene $j$ 的：

For cluster A vs B (indicator $g_i$), gene $j$:

$$\text{Z (detection)}: \quad \text{logit } \pi_{ij} = \alpha_{0j} + \alpha_{1j} g_i + \alpha_{2j} \text{CDR}_i$$ $$\text{Y (expression)}: \quad Y_{ij} | Z_{ij}=1 = \beta_{0j} + \beta_{1j} g_i + \beta_{2j} \text{CDR}_i + \varepsilon$$
CDR = cellular detection rate = 該細胞偵測到 gene 的比例（內建 confounder 校正）
CDR = cellular detection rate = fraction of detected genes per cell (built-in confounder)

10.2 Likelihood ratio test

10.2 Likelihood ratio test

合併兩部分得 χ² LRT：

Combine two parts via χ² LRT:

$$\Lambda = -2 \log \frac{\mathcal{L}(\hat{\theta}_{H_0})}{\mathcal{L}(\hat{\theta}_{H_1})} = \Lambda_Z + \Lambda_Y \sim \chi^2_{\text{df}}$$
df = 兩 part 各自的 df 加總
df = sum of two parts' degrees of freedom

這就是 MAST 同時報「detection p-value」+「expression p-value」+「joint p-value」的數學基礎。

This is why MAST reports "detection p-value" + "expression p-value" + "joint p-value" simultaneously.

§11 Pseudobulk 完整流程：Squair 2021 的 benchmark 警告Pseudobulk full pipeline: Squair 2021 benchmark warning

Squair et al. (2021) 嚴重警告：對「多 sample 比較」(疾病 vs 健康)，cell-level DE 假陽性率高達 40%。 必須用 pseudobulk。本節展示為何 + 完整流程。

Squair et al. (2021) seriously warned: for multi-sample comparisons (disease vs health), cell-level DE has 40% FPR. Must use pseudobulk. Why + full pipeline.

11.1 為什麼 cell-level DE 失敗

11.1 Why cell-level DE fails

問題：cells within one sample 高度相關（共同 patient genetics, sample handling, batch noise）。 DE test 假設 cells 獨立，所以「樣本內變異」被誤認為「真生物訊號」→ 大量假陽性。 1 個 patient 有 5,000 cells → 等於有 5,000 個「獨立」replicates，但實際只有 1 個！

Problem: cells within one sample are highly correlated (shared patient genetics, sample handling, batch noise). DE test assumes independence — "within-sample variance" gets mistaken for "real biology" → many false positives. 1 patient × 5,000 cells = looks like 5,000 "independent" replicates but actually 1!

11.2 pseudobulk 解法

11.2 Pseudobulk solution

import decoupler as dc, anndata as ad
from pydeseq2.dds import DeseqDataSet
from pydeseq2.ds import DeseqStats

# Step 1: aggregate cells -> sample × cluster pseudobulks
pb = dc.get_pseudobulk(
    adata,
    sample_col="patient",    # 每 patient 為一個 sample
    groups_col="leiden",    # 每 cluster 各別 pseudobulk
    mode="sum"
)
# shape: (n_patients × n_clusters, n_genes)

# Step 2: 對每 cluster 跑 DESeq2
for cluster in pb.obs["leiden"].unique():
    sub = pb[pb.obs["leiden"] == cluster]
    dds = DeseqDataSet(counts=sub.X, metadata=sub.obs, design_factors="condition")
    dds.deseq2()
    res = DeseqStats(dds, contrast=["condition", "disease", "control"])
    res.summary()
